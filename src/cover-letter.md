2026-11-20

To the Editor-in-Chief\
Advanced Biomedical Engineering

Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled **"Quantifying the Tremendous Hunch with a
Purpose-Built Apparatus and a New Dimensional Scale"** for consideration as an Original Paper in
Advanced Biomedical Engineering.

The tremendous hunch is reported often enough to need no defence as a phenomenon, yet no instrument
records it and no scale expresses it. We describe an apparatus that logs three component quantities,
a dimensional scale built from them, and a 20-trial evaluation in which the ordering of three
interaction conditions held in every trial while the absolute scores moved by an order of magnitude.
The work combines an engineering instrument with a measurement problem taken from human subjects,
which is why we believe it suits the interdisciplinary scope of the journal.

This manuscript has not been published previously, in whole or in part and in any language, and is
not under consideration for publication elsewhere. No related manuscript is under review, in press,
or published.

As described in the Materials and methods, the authors used a non-generative AI tool only to check the
spelling and grammar of the manuscript; no generative AI tool was used.

All authors have read and approved the submitted version.

Sincerely,
